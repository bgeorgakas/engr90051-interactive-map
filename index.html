    What these results mean

    We check whether your map pin falls inside each shaded area, even if you hide it. This does not tell you whether a property will flood, how deep the water could be, or what is happening now.

    The “2100” area comes from a planning map, not a prediction for 2100. The “2010” area is a made-up example based on that map, not a record of the 2010 flood. Comparing them does not show the effects of climate change.

    A location outside the shaded areas may still flood. If data is missing, we cannot check the location. The Elizabeth Street catchment outline shows the project area, not where complete flood information is available.

    The separate official flood-model datasets are not used in this version. Infrastructure markers and flood stories add background information; they do not affect your result or tell you that a property is protected.

        Data sources and limitations

        The original names are retained for the team's prototype. They are not verification of a year, historical event or 1% AEP probability.

        These layers do not provide water depth, a risk rating or real-time conditions. Address lookup checks whether a single approximate point intersects these same prototype shapes.

        Outside a shaded area does not mean safe. Differences between the prototype layers are not evidence of climate change or historical flooding.

        The separate official baseline and 2100 snapshots remain archived in the repository but are not loaded or used by this page.
